Settings

Fast (125 ms) follows quick sounds like claps; Slow (1 s) gives a steadier reading. Phone mics are estimates (about ±5 dB). Voice Coach: choose "Near speaker" when the phone is about an arm's length away, or "Across room" when it sits where the listeners are. Compare with a known meter and move Calibration until they match. Settings are saved on this phone.
